# Scout

## Best Runner (Primary)

> Grants infinite stamina.

- **Cooldown:** 60s
- **Duration:** 15s

Keeps your stamina full for the duration, letting you sprint without limit.

## Treasure Sense (Secondary)

> Reveals valuable items around you.

- **Cooldown:** 60s
- **Radius:** 15m

Instantly reveals (discovers) every valuable item within range.
