# Mage

## Phantom (Primary)

> Become undetectable by enemies, floating like a ghost.

- **Cooldown:** 60s
- **Duration:** 15s

Makes you undetectable by enemies and gives you a floating, zero-gravity
effect for the duration.

> **Note:** Phantom only prevents **new** enemy detections; it
> doesn't un-taunt enemies that are already chasing you when the skill is
> used.

## Reinforce (Secondary)

> Makes the item you're currently holding unbreakable.

- **Cooldown:** 90s

Makes the item you're currently holding indestructible for as long as you
keep holding it. Fails if you aren't holding anything.
