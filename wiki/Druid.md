# Druid

## Nature's Blessing (Primary)

> Heal nearby allies within a small radius.

- **Cooldown:** 45s
- **Radius:** 5m
- **Total health pool:** 50

Heals you and any ally within range, sharing a fixed pool of 50 health
between everyone who still needs healing. Players already at full health
don't consume a share of the pool.

## Raise Dead (Secondary)

> Sacrifice your own health to revive yourself, or a fallen ally.

- **Cooldown:** 120s
- **Health sacrificed:** 25

Revives yourself if downed, or revives a fallen ally whose severed head
you're physically holding, at the cost of some of your own health.
