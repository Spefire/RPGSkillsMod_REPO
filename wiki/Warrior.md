# Warrior

## Berserk (Primary)

> Increases your strength.

- **Cooldown:** 90s
- **Duration:** 15s
- **Strength levels:** +20 (grab & throw strength)

Temporarily upgrades your grab and throw strength using the same mechanism
as the in-game shop strength upgrades, so it stacks reliably and reverts
cleanly once the duration ends.

## Predator Sense (Secondary)

> Marks nearby enemies on the map, revealing them to every player.

- **Cooldown:** 60s
- **Radius:** 30m
- **Duration:** 20s

Reveals every enemy within range on the map, for all players, for the
duration.
