# Paladin

## Divine Taunt (Primary)

> Grants invulnerability, but forces nearby enemies to focus you.

- **Cooldown:** 60s
- **Duration:** 10s
- **Radius:** 15m

Grants you temporary invulnerability to all damage, and forces every enemy
within range to target you for the duration.

## Shockwave (Secondary)

> Releases a powerful shockwave, stunning all nearby enemies.

- **Cooldown:** 75s
- **Radius:** 10m
- **Stun duration:** 5s

Pushes back and briefly stuns every enemy within range.
